# 3D model sources and licenses

The simulator loads local, browser-optimized GLB assets. Original attribution metadata is retained in GLB `asset.extras` whenever the source file provided it.

Every file here has also been packed for the web by `scripts/pack-models.mjs` (an adaptation of each model): vertices reordered for the GPU's cache, geometry compressed with meshoptimizer (`EXT_meshopt_compression`, with its exponential, octahedral-normal and quaternion filters: positions kept to about 1/65 000 of the model's size), animation keyframes that their neighbours' interpolation already gives dropped, unused tangents removed, and PNG/JPEG textures re-encoded as WebP. The shapes, rigs, clips and materials are unchanged.

- `yacht-sailboat-pbr.glb` — [“Catamaran”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/catamaran-935e9e6e370447328a706b3f59dd5329) by vtillettdive, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Adaptation: the source scene/water planes were removed, geometry was simplified from 371,675 to 162,913 triangles, and vertex data was quantized. The finished asset retains the twin hulls, mast, curved mainsail, boom, rigging, cockpit, helm and canopy.
- `shark-animated.glb` — `shark.glb` from the [BabylonJS Meshes Library](https://github.com/BabylonJS/MeshesLibrary), covered by that library's default [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) terms. The 51,199-triangle rig includes authored `swimming`, `circling`, and `bite` clips. Adaptation: textures resized to 1K WebP and geometry/animation data quantized. At runtime the material, which has no base colour, is countershaded (dark back, pale belly) in the shader; the `swimming` clip is driven by the simulator's tail-beat phase and effort, and the spine is bent into turns procedurally. Beyond that and the web packing above, the file is unchanged.
- `blue-whale-rigged-pbr-v2.glb` — [“Blue Whale - Textured”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/blue-whale-textured-d24d19021c724c3a9134eebcb76b0e0f) by Bohdan Lvov, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Includes the authored `Swimming` clip. Adaptation: the rigged PBR mesh was Loop-subdivided twice from 2,424 to 38,784 triangles, tangents and smooth normals were rebuilt, textures were converted to WebP, and geometry/animation data was quantized. The versioned filename forces clients to fetch the volumetric model instead of a stale earlier whale asset. The simulator rejects whale assets without a detailed skinned mesh and a valid swim clip rather than creating a procedural fallback. At runtime only the clip's flipper and eye tracks are played; the body pitch and the tail joints `locator4`–`locator6` are posed procedurally.
- `tropical-fish-school.glb` — [“Animated Swimming Tropical Fish School Loop”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-swimming-tropical-fish-school-loop-62ccf83b35c744d7b5ffb7be80d4ea99) by LasquetiSpice, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The asset contains four textured species sharing an authored 41-second animation. Adaptation: 512px WebP textures and quantized geometry/animation data. At runtime the simulator extracts one fish of each species from the rigged school and renders schools of instances of it, animated by a procedural swimming body wave instead of the authored loop.
- `seagull-animated.glb` — [“Flying Seagull”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/flying-seagull-07dde3ea7d9048588d3c4edfd37ac20d) by The lighthouse keeper / geminga, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Includes authored `flap`, `planer`, and `planer 2` clips. This optimized adaptation remains CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `dolphin-animated.glb` — [“Dolphin Animated”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dolphin-animated-1049ca2426d74280831ef3bf9f2dbc57) by Akshat / shooter24994, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The smooth 3,728-triangle rig contains separate body, belly, mouth and eye materials plus a three-second authored swim cycle. At runtime the model is scaled by its length (2.7 m) rather than its fin span, the body material is toned from teal to slate grey, only the clip's fin tracks are played, and the spine and flukes are posed procedurally.
- `trader-man.glb`, `trader-woman.glb`, `trader-pirate.glb` — the user's own rigged character models (`man-base-character-rigged`, `woman-base-character-rigged`, `pirate-rigged`), simplified from the originals with meshoptimizer to about 110k triangles each, keeping the authored normals (smooth faces, no facets) and textures at 2K colour, 1.5K normal and 1K metal-roughness. The island traders; `trader-man` is also the skipper at the wheel (`RiggedSkipper`) and casts the walker's own shadow ashore (`WalkerShadow`).
- `crab-red.glb`, `crab-blue.glb` — the user's crab models (`crab.glb`, `blue-crab.glb`), the seabed and beach crabs.
- `motoryacht-35.glb` — [“motoryacht 35”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/motoryacht-35-0bdd7a0de7254426890bb5745bb7da6d) by angelo raffaele catalano, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), supplied by the user as the prologue's yacht (an open cruiser with a real helm under the hardtop). Adaptation: welded and simplified from 1.54 million to 115,758 triangles, specular-glossiness materials converted to metal-roughness, web packing. At runtime (`ModelCrafts`) she is scaled to 11.4 m and turned bow-forward, her topsides and hardtop whitened to the story's livery, her ensign and its staff removed, the network's alert drawn on her chart screen; blackened copies of her materials make the wreck on the reef.
- `sport-yacht.glb` — [“Speed Boat”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/speed-boat-763d63511abd44e4bae99509e18d00fa) by gogiart, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), supplied by the user for the last chapter (no longer the prologue's yacht, see `motoryacht-35.glb`; not loaded yet). Adaptation: transmission dropped, parts joined per material, geometry welded and simplified from 752,000 to 113,709 triangles, textures resized to at most 1024 px WebP. At runtime (`ModelCrafts`) a stray line primitive is dropped, the gelcoat is given its gloss, the red of the livery is whitened to the story's white hull with the black band, and a flybridge helm (console, wheel, display, seat) is added on her hardtop; blackened copies of her materials make the wreck on the reef.
- `skiff-center-console.glb` — a 6 m centre-console boat by Innerscene Corp (innerscene.com), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), supplied by the user as the skipper's boat after the prologue. Adaptation: the web packing above. At runtime its glazing is made clear and tinted, and its outboard turns with the helm.
- `view-pistol.glb` — [“9 mm Pistol handgun with animation”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9-mm-pistol-handgun-with-animation-302ee46ebd8943aa99ca13cf61f42a32) by dzubei777, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), supplied by the user as the skipper's pistol in first person. Adaptation: textures resized to WebP, geometry packed for the web; the authored `Armature|shoot` clip is kept. At runtime (`WeaponView.setModels`) it replaces the procedural pistol in the hands built in code, its shot clip plays with each shot, and it is turned over in a straight arm when Mark hands it over (`WeaponView.inspect`).
- `view-rifle.glb` — [“AKM reload animation”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/akm-reload-animation-5e5b027e946c45658eea4342876645f8) by Vlasov Daniil, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), supplied by the user as the skipper's rifle in first person. Adaptation: its first-person arms (their meshes bound in another unit than the gun's, which drew them torn apart in three.js) and the parallax sight insert were removed, textures resized to WebP, geometry packed; the authored `Reload` clip is kept and driven by the reload's progress. The hands are the ones built in code, placed at the grip and the fore-end.
- `fig-mark.glb` — Mark Holloway: the user's `Mark.glb` (a Character Creator figure exported from Blender; its source and licence were not recorded in the file and are to be confirmed by the user). Adaptation: the 22 body bones renamed to the game's figure names (`FIGURE_BONES`), animations, morph targets (205 facial shapes), vertex colours (Character Creator's masks, which three.js would multiply into the skin and turn the face black), the eye-occlusion and tear-line shells (they need Character Creator's own shaders) removed; skin, nails and teeth given non-metallic materials; textures 768 px WebP. 241 MB → 2.9 MB.
- `fig-guard-a.glb` — a Vanguard soldier: [“Soldier fully rigged character”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/soldier-fully-rigged-character-d5ee1b51099741d2b5e4010f4f781263) by Bad boy for life, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), supplied by the user (`enemy1.glb`). Adaptation: Mixamo bones renamed to `FIGURE_BONES`, animations removed, its specular-glossiness materials converted to metal-roughness (three.js no longer reads the old extension and drew it white), textures ≤ 1024 px WebP.
- `fig-guard-b.glb` — a Vanguard soldier: [“Soldier, blender rigged”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/soldier-blender-rigged-89049b924243477aabff7d85dba06c48) by samuel.brunner, [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) (non-commercial use only), supplied by the user (`enemy2.glb`). Adaptation: bones renamed to `FIGURE_BONES`, animations removed, textures ≤ 1024 px WebP.

The three figures are made ready when they load (`AssetManager`, `actors/FigureAdapt.ts`): stood facing +Z on the ground at 1.8 m (`standFigure`, measured on the skinned mesh), and given the islanders' walk and run retargeted onto their own skeletons (`retargetGait`: each bone turned from its rest by the rotation the islander's bone makes from his, after the two rests are brought to one stance; the head keeps its own rest). `ActorBody` draws them as authored: no repaint of the base figure's texture and no kit on the bones.

- `yacht-quaternius.glb` — legacy, unused fallback [“Sail Ship”](https://www.get3dmodels.com/vehicles/sail-ship-low-poly/) by Quaternius, Public Domain [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Ships (`public/models/ships/`)

All eight are [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) models from Sketchfab, downloaded as GLB on 2026-10-18. Adaptation for every file: the author's sea and ground planes and stray props were removed, transmission glass was made opaque, parts were merged per material (and untextured materials into a colour palette), geometry was welded, simplified where heavy (meshoptimizer) and quantized (`KHR_mesh_quantization`), and textures were resized to at most 1024 px WebP. At runtime each model is turned bow forward, scaled to its type's length and floated at its waterline (`SHIP_MODELS` in `src/simulator/ships/ShipModelMath.ts`).

- The folded blades of dune grass and seagrass and the branching coral colonies are original Coral Pass geometry generated by `desktop/Tools/coastal_geometry.py`. Their colors, translucency, caustics, and wind or current motion are authored in the desktop materials.
