# 3D model sources and licenses

The simulator loads local, browser-optimized GLB assets. Original attribution metadata is retained in GLB `asset.extras` whenever the source file provided it.

- `yacht-sailboat-pbr.glb` — [“Catamaran”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/catamaran-935e9e6e370447328a706b3f59dd5329) by vtillettdive, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Adaptation: the source scene/water planes were removed, geometry was simplified from 371,675 to 162,913 triangles, and vertex data was quantized. The finished asset retains the twin hulls, mast, curved mainsail, boom, rigging, cockpit, helm and canopy.
- `shark-animated.glb` — `shark.glb` from the [BabylonJS Meshes Library](https://github.com/BabylonJS/MeshesLibrary), covered by that library's default [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) terms. The 51,199-triangle rig includes authored `swimming`, `circling`, and `bite` clips. Adaptation: textures resized to 1K WebP and geometry/animation data quantized. At runtime the material, which has no base colour, is countershaded (dark back, pale belly) in the shader; the `swimming` clip is driven by the simulator's tail-beat phase and effort, and the spine is bent into turns procedurally. The file itself is unchanged.
- `blue-whale-rigged-pbr-v2.glb` — [“Blue Whale - Textured”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/blue-whale-textured-d24d19021c724c3a9134eebcb76b0e0f) by Bohdan Lvov, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Includes the authored `Swimming` clip. Adaptation: the rigged PBR mesh was Loop-subdivided twice from 2,424 to 38,784 triangles, tangents and smooth normals were rebuilt, textures were converted to WebP, and geometry/animation data was quantized. The versioned filename forces clients to fetch the volumetric model instead of a stale earlier whale asset. The simulator rejects whale assets without a detailed skinned mesh and a valid swim clip rather than creating a procedural fallback. At runtime only the clip's flipper and eye tracks are played; the body pitch and the tail joints `locator4`–`locator6` are posed procedurally.
- `tropical-fish-school.glb` — [“Animated Swimming Tropical Fish School Loop”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-swimming-tropical-fish-school-loop-62ccf83b35c744d7b5ffb7be80d4ea99) by LasquetiSpice, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The asset contains four textured species sharing an authored 41-second animation. Adaptation: 512px WebP textures and quantized geometry/animation data. At runtime the simulator extracts one fish of each species from the rigged school and renders schools of instances of it, animated by a procedural swimming body wave instead of the authored loop.
- `seagull-animated.glb` — [“Flying Seagull”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/flying-seagull-07dde3ea7d9048588d3c4edfd37ac20d) by The lighthouse keeper / geminga, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Includes authored `flap`, `planer`, and `planer 2` clips. This optimized adaptation remains CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `dolphin-animated.glb` — [“Dolphin Animated”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dolphin-animated-1049ca2426d74280831ef3bf9f2dbc57) by Akshat / shooter24994, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The smooth 3,728-triangle rig contains separate body, belly, mouth and eye materials plus a three-second authored swim cycle. At runtime the model is scaled by its length (2.7 m) rather than its fin span, the body material is toned from teal to slate grey, only the clip's fin tracks are played, and the spine and flukes are posed procedurally.
- `yacht-quaternius.glb` — legacy, unused fallback [“Sail Ship”](https://www.get3dmodels.com/vehicles/sail-ship-low-poly/) by Quaternius, Public Domain [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Ships (`public/models/ships/`)

All eight are [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) models from Sketchfab, downloaded as GLB on 2026-10-18. Adaptation for every file: the author's sea and ground planes and stray props were removed, transmission glass was made opaque, parts were merged per material (and untextured materials into a colour palette), geometry was welded, simplified where heavy (meshoptimizer) and quantized (`KHR_mesh_quantization`), and textures were resized to at most 1024 px WebP. At runtime each model is turned bow forward, scaled to its type's length and floated at its waterline (`SHIP_MODELS` in `src/simulator/ships/ShipModelMath.ts`).
